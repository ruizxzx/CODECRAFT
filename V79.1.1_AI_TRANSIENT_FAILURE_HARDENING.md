# V79.1.1 — AI Transient-Failure Hardening

- Adds exponential-backoff retries with jitter for transient Gemini 503/5xx responses.
- Falls back across configured/current Flash models when a model is temporarily overloaded.
- Adds client-side retry for the transient Gemini busy response.
- Applies the same resilience to discussion intelligence.
- Deduplicates identical error toasts during rapid failures.
- Preserves Firebase authentication, grounding, caching, quotas and core-content independence from Gemini.
