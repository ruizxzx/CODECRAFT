Cumulative production release built on OFFSCRPT V71 Article Version History.

- V71 article revision history preserved.
- Creator analytics with 7D / 30D / 90D / All Time filters, aggregate reader metrics, article comparison and reading funnel.
- Behavior-driven recommendations from cloud reading history, followed creators/topics, saves and reactions.
- Nested article comments with replies, editing, deletion, reporting, moderation, mentions, reactions and sorting.
- Canonical universal share/deep-link system for articles, series, profiles, posts and topics.
- Granular moderator permissions enforced by UI and Firestore rules.
- Firestore-backed user reporting and moderation queue support.
- Efficient 30-second community presence heartbeat with expiry.
- Admin-only System Health Center with real Firebase/Auth/Firestore/PWA/runtime checks and explicit unverified states.
