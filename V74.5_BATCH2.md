# OFFSCRPT V74.5 — Batch 2 Recommendation Engine

Implemented the left-out Recommendation Engine roadmap items: full user signal collection, weighted scoring, read/completed penalties, creator/topic/series diversity, recommendation explanations, cold-start topic seeding, cloud search behavior capture, and realtime signal subscriptions.

Firestore is the source of truth for signed-in personalization signals. Series follow writes are mirrored into users/{uid}/followedSeries so the recommendation engine can subscribe without collection-group scans.

No recommendation record is hardcoded; article candidates come from current published content and user signals come from Firestore.


Additional integrity: article reaction state is mirrored to users/{uid}/reactionIndex, so reaction signals are cloud-readable without broad collection-group queries. Series follows are mirrored to users/{uid}/followedSeries. Search events are protected by account-scoped rules.
