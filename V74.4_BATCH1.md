# OFFSCRPT V74.4 — Creator Analytics Completion

This release completes the remaining Creator Analytics foundation work identified in the roadmap.

## Included
- 6M and 1Y creator analytics ranges.
- Firestore range queries for time-bounded analytics.
- Selectable article comparison (up to four articles).
- Aggregate reading funnel with clickable stages and retention from the previous stage.
- Series analytics per-part opens, 50%+ readers, completed readers, completion rate, and retention/drop-off.
- Cloud-synced search behavior capture as groundwork for recommendation signals.

## Data integrity
Analytics are derived from Firestore session/events. No creator control exists to edit metric values. Failed cloud writes are not converted into successful-looking analytics.

## Next batches
Recommendation completion, professional comments completion, sharing/deep links, moderator completion, reporting completion, presence completion, and full system-health completion remain separate batches.
